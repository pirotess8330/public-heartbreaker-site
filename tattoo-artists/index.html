More about Erik “Epik” Starr

Epik has been tattooing for 25 yrs. With a passion for creating tattoos that stand the test of time, he will design your custom tattoo with all your ideas in mind. He started his tattoo journey in Texas and since he has tattooed in New Mexico, Colorado, California, Hawaii, Oregon as well as in Guadalajara, Mexico City. Juarez, and other places, following the convention circuit around the world. He successfully ran the southwest’s iconic Cosmic Ink Tattoo Studio for a decade producing incredible works of art. He's now tattooing here at Heartbreaker Tattoo! Epik Starr is here to make your custom tattoo dreams a reality.
