Booking & visiting

What ID do I need? Can a minor get pierced or tattooed?

Call before booking to confirm the age and ID requirements for your specific service. For a minor’s piercing, ask which placements are offered and what documentation the parent or legal guardian and minor need to bring.

Tattoo designs & styles

Can you design a custom tattoo for me?

Yes. Bring a rough idea, sketches, or reference photos, and tell us which details you love. Your artist can help turn that into a design sized and shaped for your placement.

Explore custom tattoos
What tattoo styles can I ask about?

Traditional uses bold outlines and classic motifs. Neo-traditional adds more detail and color. Realism uses shading to build lifelike subjects. Fine line emphasizes delicate lines, geometric work uses structured shapes, and watercolor uses soft washes of color.

Explore tattoo styles
How do I choose a tattoo artist?

Look at completed work in the style you want. Pay attention to the artist’s linework, shading, color, and experience with the placement or subject you have in mind.

Browse artist portfolios
What is flash? Can I choose an available design?

Flash is artwork the artist has already drawn for tattooing. The available-design galleries are a good place to start. Ask the artist if your design is still available and which sizes or placements work for it.

See artists and available designs
Can you cover up or rework an old tattoo?

Yes. Send a clear photo in natural light and include its size and placement. The color, density, and shape of the old tattoo affect your options. A cover-up may need to be larger or darker in specific areas.

See cover-up examples
How do I choose size and placement?

Think about how visible you want the piece to be, how it will move with your body, and how much detail you want. Your artist can suggest a size that gives small details enough room and place the stencil so you can see it on your body.

Can I get tattooed over a scar?

Ask for a consultation first. The age, texture, and location of a scar affect what’s possible. If it’s still healing or you’re prone to raised scars, ask a healthcare professional before booking.

Tattoo prices & sessions

How much will my tattoo cost?

Price depends on the size, placement, complexity, artist, and time involved. Send your design idea, approximate dimensions, placement, and reference photos for a quote. The journal explains what goes into pricing.

Read the tattoo cost guide
Do you have a tattoo minimum or require a deposit?

Ask your artist for the current minimum, whether a deposit is required, and how it applies to the quote. Confirm cancellation and rescheduling terms before paying.

What are your Tap Out sessions and how much do they cost?

Tap Out sessions are longer bookings for tattoo projects that need more chair time. The current options are 6 hours for $700, 8 hours for $900, and 10 hours for $1,100. Ask your artist how much of your project may fit in one session.

Explore Tap Out sessions
How long will my tattoo take?

A small, simple piece usually takes less time than dense shading, full color, or a large design. A sleeve or other big project may need several appointments. Get an estimate once the artist knows the design and placement.

Do you offer touch-ups?

Contact your artist once the tattoo has healed. Send a clear photo and ask whether a touch-up is recommended, when to book, and what it costs.

Before & during a tattoo

How should I prepare for my first tattoo?

Get a good night’s sleep, eat a meal before your appointment, drink water, and wear clothes that give easy access to the area. Bring your references and any identification the studio asks for.

Read the first tattoo guide
Does getting tattooed hurt?

Most people feel scratching or stinging. The sensation varies by placement, session length, and the person. Areas over bone can feel more intense. Tell your artist if you need a break.

What happens during the appointment?

You’ll review the design and placement with your artist. For a stencil-based design, you’ll see where it sits on your body before tattooing starts. Speak up if you’d like an adjustment. Your artist will explain the dressing and aftercare afterward.

What if I’m nervous about needles or feel faint?

Tell your artist before you start if you have a history of feeling faint. If you feel dizzy, nauseated, or lightheaded during the session, tell them right away so they can pause.

Can I use numbing cream before I come in?

Ask your artist before applying anything. Numbing products can affect the skin and some can cause serious reactions when misused. Follow product directions and ask a clinician or pharmacist about safe use if needed.

Tattoo healing & aftercare

How do I take care of a fresh tattoo?

Follow the instructions for the dressing your artist uses. Wash your hands before touching the area. Once the dressing comes off as directed, gently clean the tattoo, pat it dry, and use the aftercare product your artist recommends.

Read the tattoo healing guide
How long does a tattoo take to heal?

The surface often looks better within a few weeks, while deeper skin can take longer. Healing varies with size, placement, and your skin. Follow your artist’s instructions even if the tattoo looks healed early.

Is peeling or itching normal?

Some itching and flaking can happen during healing. Let flakes come off on their own and avoid picking or scratching. If symptoms get worse or you develop a rash, get medical advice.

Can I shower, swim, or work out with a fresh tattoo?

Follow your dressing instructions for showers. Avoid soaking in baths, pools, or hot tubs until the tattoo heals. Ask your artist when to resume workouts that rub or stretch the area.

How do I protect my tattoo from the sun?

Keep a fresh tattoo out of direct sun while it heals. After it’s healed, protect it with clothing or broad-spectrum sunscreen.

When should I seek medical care for a healing tattoo?

Seek medical advice promptly for spreading redness, worsening pain or warmth, pus, red streaks, or fever.

What piercings can I get?

What happens during a piercing appointment?

Your piercer can check your anatomy, discuss jewelry, mark the placement, and review it with you before proceeding. Ask them to cover aftercare, expected healing time, and whether you’ll need a follow-up or a shorter post.

Piercing healing & aftercare

How do I clean a new piercing?

Follow your piercer’s instructions. Wash your hands first, use sterile saline wound wash as directed, and avoid twisting the jewelry or using harsh cleaners.

Read professional piercing aftercare guidance
How long does a piercing take to heal?

Healing depends on the placement. Earlobes often heal sooner than cartilage, nipples, or navels. Ask your piercer for a timeline for your specific piercing and a check before you change the jewelry.

When can I change or downsize my jewelry?

Wait for your piercer’s guidance. Some piercings need a shorter post after swelling subsides, and the piercer can do that before the piercing is ready for you to change jewelry yourself.

Can I swim, sleep on it, or wear headphones?

Avoid submerging a healing piercing in pools, hot tubs, or natural water. Avoid pressure or friction from sleeping on it, tight clothing, headphones, or helmets where they contact the site.

What if I have a bump, swelling, or possible infection?

Ask your piercer to check the fit and placement if you’re experiencing irritation. Get prompt medical advice for worsening heat, pain, or redness, pus, or fever. If infection is possible, ask a healthcare professional before removing jewelry.
